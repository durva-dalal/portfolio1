Case Study · University of Leeds

O2 Telefonica Weather Hijack

Brand strategy for reaching 16 to 30 year olds who ignore telecoms advertising through weather-reactive digital billboards and contextual copy.

The challenge

Create a telecom campaign that young audiences would not tune out, by using context and relevance rather than generic category messaging.

The idea

“Together, Everywhere” used live weather conditions to trigger relevant outdoor copy, making the campaign feel less like interruption and more like presence.
